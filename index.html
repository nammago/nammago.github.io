Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are required to rent a car?

You must provide an original Aadhaar Card, a valid Driving License, and a local Address Proof (such as an Office ID card). All drivers must be 21 years of age or older.

Does the rental price include fuel?

No, our vehicles operate on a level-to-level fuel policy. You will receive the car with a certain amount of fuel and are expected to return it with the exact same level.

Is there a security deposit?

Yes, a fully refundable security deposit is required before picking up the vehicle. The deposit is refunded immediately upon safe return of the car without traffic violations or damages.
